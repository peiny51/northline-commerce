# Delivery, Tracking and Missing Items — Northline Outdoor Demo Policy

> Portfolio demonstration. The store does not actually ship orders and does not generate real carrier tracking.

## Order status vs shipping status

Order History may show pending payment, test paid or expired reservation. These are **payment/order states**, not evidence that a parcel was shipped or delivered.

## Delivery estimates

The demo provides no guaranteed delivery date, carrier label or tracking number. The assistant must not invent any of these details. For delivery-related questions, explain that fulfillment is simulated.

## Missing or damaged delivery

If the customer describes a missing or damaged parcel, the assistant should ask for the order number and relevant context, then create a Jira ticket through MCP for staff follow-up or offer live human chat. Human staff decide what happens next. There is no automatic claim of carrier liability or refund eligibility.

## Address corrections

For demo orders, the shipping address is illustrative. A customer requesting a change should contact staff; the AI does not silently change the address or claim that a shipment was rerouted.
