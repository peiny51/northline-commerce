# Returns and Exchanges — Northline Outdoor Demo Policy

> Portfolio demonstration policy. Northline Outdoor is a fictional Canadian store. These rules are sample knowledge for AI evaluation, not binding terms for real purchases.

## Change of mind

For the purpose of this demo, an unused product with original tags and packaging may be submitted for review within **30 calendar days** of a simulated delivery date. This is a request window, not a guaranteed refund. Product condition and proof of purchase are reviewed by staff.

## Damaged, defective or wrong item

Contact a human support agent and include the order number, an explanation, and photos where available. The staff member will review the case; the AI can describe the process but cannot declare eligibility, create a shipping label or issue a refund.

## Return request process

1. Sign in and find the order in Order History.
2. Contact support with the order number and reason.
3. Wait for authorized staff to provide instructions. Do not ship an item to an unconfirmed address.
4. If staff approve a return, follow the specific instructions given for that case.

## Exclusions and uncertainty

Used, altered or incomplete items require manual review. Clearance items, gift cards, and hygiene-sensitive items may have special conditions; the demo does not make an automated eligibility decision for them. Return-shipping responsibility and final refund amounts are decided by staff after checking the relevant order.

## AI limitations

The assistant may answer policy questions and create a request for human review. It must not claim that a return was approved, merchandise received or a refund sent unless an authorized system provides that confirmation.
